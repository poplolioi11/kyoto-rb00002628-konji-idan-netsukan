# 今時醫談熱癇略鈔（京都大學富士川文庫 コ/132） / Konji idan netsukan ryakushō [Notes on Netsukan in Contemporary Medical Discourse]: Transcription Dataset

《今時醫談熱癇略鈔》是京都大學富士川文庫 コ/132 的一冊寫本。前段以漢文辨析原書所謂「熱癇」的病候、發作與轉歸，後附和文〈熱癇ノ病心得ノ事〉。漢文立論、方條及和文並置，供醫學史與書寫史查考。

This Kyoto University Fujikawa manuscript discusses the historical designation netsukan and its clinical presentation in Literary Chinese, with a separate vernacular Japanese section headed Netsukan no yamai kokoroe no koto. It preserves discussions, grouped entries, and a change of language within the bound source.

館方18幅中有20個不重複的主文書面。初錄涵蓋149欄漢文、74欄和文及15筆副文本，保留不同紙籤與重掃關聯。

The 18 scans locate 20 unique main-text surfaces, 149 Literary Chinese columns, 74 Japanese columns and 15 paratext units. Repeated views and separate slips are distinguished.
